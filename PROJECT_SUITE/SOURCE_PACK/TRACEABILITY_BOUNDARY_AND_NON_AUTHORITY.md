# Traceability Boundary and Non-Authority

This slice is a non-sovereign Reference Implementation against the active harmonized constitutional and implementation draft baseline.

Trace records, mappings, findings, evidence references, deviation references, and validation results are descriptive implementation artifacts. They do not amend, adopt, interpret, supersede, or authenticate constitutional source specifications. Source-version discrepancies remain recorded as source metadata and are not normalized by the implementation.

The traceability model does not provide:

- constitutional adoption or authority;
- conformance, certification, or assurance;
- evidence sufficiency or operational proof;
- release authorization, activation, execution, or transport;
- production authorization or operational recognition; or
- authority for software to create constitutional meaning.

IMP-005 canonical representation expansion is the next planned slice. No canonical trace serialization is introduced here. IMP-006 assurance, IMP-007 evidence/provenance expansion, and later release/activation/operational domains remain deferred.
