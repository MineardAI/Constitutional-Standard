# Verification and Assurance Non-Authority

The Phase 13 component evaluates declared records only. It does not:

- admit or interpret constitutional sources;
- create or modify requirements;
- establish constitutional truth, validity, or authority;
- execute tests, components, acts, or runtime systems;
- own, certify, or determine evidence sufficiency;
- determine conformance or certification;
- authorize release, deployment, activation, or operational recognition;
- persist records or expose transport/network services; or
- infer correctness from canonical decoding, traceability completeness, or test count.

All result structures expose explicit non-claims and keep authority, conformance, certification, release, activation, operational-recognition, evidence-ownership, and execution flags false.
