# Evidence Origin, Provenance, and Custody Model

Origin identifies source, producer, collector, method, time, and environment. Provenance preserves attributable events and derivation inputs. Lineage identifies source evidence, derivation method, and conditions. Custody records controlled possession and transfers separately from provenance and ownership.

Gaps and limitations remain representable. No transfer, provenance event, or custody record establishes authenticity, truth, authority, or permitted reliance automatically.
