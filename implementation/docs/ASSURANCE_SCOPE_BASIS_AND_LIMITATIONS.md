# Assurance Scope, Basis, and Limitations

An assurance record identifies one assessment, explicit verified scope, implementation version, source binding, assessment basis, outcome, status, canonical binding, unresolved finding references, and non-empty limitations.

Assurance cannot be issued when its assessment basis is missing, its scope exceeds the assessment scope, source/version bindings conflict, traceability or evidence references are malformed, required inputs are absent, blocking findings remain unresolved, or prohibited authority claims are present.

Assurance statuses are represented as `Issued`, `Current`, `Suspended`, `Superseded`, `Withdrawn`, `Expired`, and `Archived`. Status does not alter historical assessment content and does not expand the declared scope.

Assurance is not conformance, certification, release authorization, activation eligibility, operational recognition, evidence sufficiency, constitutional truth, or general correctness.
