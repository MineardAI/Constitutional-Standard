# Activation and Operational Recognition Realization Profile

Constitutional source: `IMP-009_Implementation_Activation_and_Operational_Recognition_v0.2.0_Candidate_Review_Draft.md` (Candidate Review Draft; status preserved).

Ownership: dedicated `constitutional-activation` crate. It realizes Operational Eligibility Determinations, Activation Decisions, Operational Assignments, Activation Acts and Effects, Operational Recognition, Operational Standing, Operational Baselines, and supporting historical records.

Activation and recognition are separate. Activation requires explicit release, target, scope, environment, context, source-set, authority, jurisdiction, authorization, eligibility, and decision bindings. Recognition requires its own request, scope, criteria, recognizing authority, and bounded activation reference where required. Neither creates authority, conformance, certification, truth, or operational effectiveness.

Suspension, withdrawal, deactivation, and reactivation are represented as distinct transitions. Canonical projections use the existing IMP-005 architecture. No deployment, runtime invocation, publication, orchestration, live infrastructure inspection, or external mutation is implemented.

Second closure remediation adds deterministic ordered-history validation, predecessor/result continuity, subject/release/assignment/version continuity, rollback non-restoration checks, recognition decision/criterion/finding/condition/limitation validation, recognition history preservation, and bounded activation traceability. These validators remain non-authoritative and non-executing.

Contract-derived remediation completes source-required assignment-bound history resolution and transition standing bindings, including suspension non-erasure and withdrawal history. Initial assignment records remain valid without predecessors; no universal lifecycle graph or automatic recognition transition is introduced.
